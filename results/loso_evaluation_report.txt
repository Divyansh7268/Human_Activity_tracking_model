Key Cross-Subject Generalization Insights:
  1. Sitting Recognition:
     Sitting achieved 99.78% F1 across unseen subjects.
     The normalized hip flexion angles and thigh orientation provide a clear, invariant signature.
  2. Standing vs. Walking Separation:
     Scale-invariant movement speed and lower-body displacement prevent standing postures
     from being confused with walking even when body dimensions and camera distances vary.
  3. Locomotion Boundary (Walking vs. Running):
     The primary confusion boundary on unseen individuals occurs between fast walking and running.
     Different people naturally jog or walk at differing cadence and limb excursion ranges.

RESEARCH INTEGRITY & LIMITATIONS:
  • Small Sample (N=4): While this demonstrates genuine subject independence without data leakage,
    a 4-subject dataset is preliminary. We do NOT claim universal human-level generalization.
  • Demographics: Additional subjects with diverse heights, clothing, and gait styles will further
    solidify the boundary distributions.
