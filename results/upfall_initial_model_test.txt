────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. INTERPRETATION & LIMITATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   • The dataset was derived from personal recordings, so these
     results primarily validate that the full pipeline (feature
     engineering → temporal features → training → evaluation) works
     end-to-end with the UP-Fall data schema.
   • A 3-class problem (no running) is simpler than the 4-class
     personal-dataset scenario.
   • To obtain generalizable multi-subject results, the actual
     UP-Fall ADL videos (A6, A7, A8) need to be processed through
     MediaPipe Pose to extract real skeleton data.
   • Do NOT cite these results as a research benchmark.
