================================================================================
TEMPORAL FEATURE ENGINEERING SUMMARY REPORT (STEP 7)
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The purpose of Step 7 was to augment the single-frame pose representation with
frame-to-frame temporal movement dynamics. In static single-frame space, standing
and walking are geometrically ambiguous. Adding velocity, displacement, and
acceleration gives the classifier the ability to distinguish whether a subject
is holding still or actively in motion.


2. TEMPORAL FEATURES CREATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eight dynamic movement features were calculated from 3D Euclidean displacements:

1. mean_landmark_displacement : Mean displacement across all 33 MediaPipe landmarks.
2. max_landmark_displacement  : Peak displacement among any single landmark.
3. upper_body_movement        : Mean displacement of head, shoulders, arms, hands (indices 0-22).
4. lower_body_movement        : Mean displacement of hips, knees, ankles, feet (indices 23-32).
5. left_side_movement         : Mean displacement across all 16 left-side landmarks.
6. right_side_movement        : Mean displacement across all 16 right-side landmarks.
7. movement_speed             : Landmark velocity in coordinate units per second (disp / dt, dt = 0.15s).
8. movement_acceleration      : Rate of change of movement speed (delta_speed / dt).


3. MATHEMATICAL CALCULATION METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Landmark coordinates were extracted for each frame as 3D points p_i(t) = (x, y, z).
- Euclidean displacement for landmark i between consecutive frames:
    d_i(t) = sqrt((x_i(t) - x_i(t-1))^2 + (y_i(t) - y_i(t-1))^2 + (z_i(t) - z_i(t-1))^2)
- Speed was scaled by the 150 ms sampling interval:
    speed(t) = mean_displacement(t) / 0.15
- Acceleration was calculated as the magnitude of velocity change:
    acceleration(t) = |speed(t) - speed(t-1)| / 0.15


4. SEQUENCE & FRAME ORDERING VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Explicit Metadata Check:
  The raw CSV (data/raw/pose_dataset.csv) does NOT contain explicit metadata columns
  such as 'timestamp', 'frame_id', or 'session_id'.
- Physical Sequence Verification:
  Analysis of row transitions revealed that the dataset consists of 4 unbroken,
  contiguous blocks:
    * Block 1: standing (rows 0-445, 446 frames)
    * Block 2: sitting  (rows 446-952, 507 frames)
    * Block 3: walking  (rows 953-1173, 221 frames)
    * Block 4: running  (rows 1174-1297, 124 frames)
  Because data was recorded in real time with a fixed 150 ms sample interval,
  rows WITHIN each block are strictly chronological and sequential.
- Sequence Boundary Handling:
  To prevent calculating invalid artificial jumps between different activities,
  displacements were computed strictly WITHIN each block. The initial boundary frame
  of each block was omitted (4 rows dropped in total), leaving 1,294 clean samples
  with valid temporal derivatives.


5. STANDING VS. WALKING SEPARATION ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standing and walking became substantially distinguishable:

* Lower-Body Movement:
  - Standing Mean: 0.0189 (std: 0.0277)
  - Walking Mean : 0.0647 (std: 0.0769)
  - Difference   : 3.42x higher in walking (Cohen's d = 0.792)

* Overall Movement Speed:
  - Standing Mean: 0.1760 units/s (std: 0.2797)
  - Walking Mean : 0.5513 units/s (std: 0.6431)
  - Difference   : 3.13x higher in walking (Cohen's d = 0.757)

* Movement Acceleration:
  - Standing Mean: 0.6475 units/s^2 (std: 1.3396)
  - Walking Mean : 2.3650 units/s^2 (std: 3.8072)
  - Difference   : 3.65x higher in walking (Cohen's d = 0.602)


6. LIMITATIONS & ISSUES TO ADDRESS BEFORE RETRAINING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. No Session ID in Collector:
   If future datasets are collected across multiple sessions or non-contiguous
   recordings, collect_pose_data.py must be updated to store a session_id or timestamp.
2. Real-Time Streaming State:
   In live prediction, the classifier will require state (caching the previous frame's
   landmarks and previous speed) to compute these 8 temporal features on the fly.
3. Outlier/Spike Filtering:
   Occasional tracking jumps in MediaPipe (e.g. foot occlusion) cause brief speed spikes.
   A rolling average or median filter will ensure smooth live speed estimation.


7. OUTPUT ARTIFACTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dataset: data/processed/temporal_features.csv (1,294 rows, 256 columns)
  (247 single-frame features + 8 temporal features + 1 activity label)
- Visual Chart: results/temporal_movement_distribution.png
