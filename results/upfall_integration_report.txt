12. ISSUES ENCOUNTERED & RESEARCH NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. UP-Fall ADL Availability in 3D Skeleton Release:
   The peer-reviewed 3D Skeleton release by Koffi et al. (Zenodo 12773013) published 82 CSV files
   corresponding only to the Fall categories (A1 to A5). Skeletons for A6 (walking), A7 (standing),
   and A8 (sitting) are not provided in the pre-extracted fall-impact archive and must be either
   batch-extracted from the original raw MP4 videos via MediaPipe or integrated via standardized
   trial CSVs as demonstrated by load_upfall.py.
2. Absence of Running:
   UP-Fall does not include a running protocol. A hybrid training dataset (augmenting UP-Fall
   with local running data) or a 3-class model will be required when training resumes.
